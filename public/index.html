What changed

Every Singapore Citizen child now receives the same support regardless of birth order.

Transition rules

For children born before 1 April 2027, who are not starting from scratch.

  • Children still receiving Baby Bonus Cash Gift payouts keep them until 31 Mar 2027, then get a top-up by 30 Apr 2027 bringing the total to $10,000.
  • Child Credits are backdated. Children who turn 1 to 16 in 2026 receive their 2026 tranche of $2,000 by 30 Apr 2027; children with January to April birthdays also get their 2027 tranche by then. From 2028 the credits arrive on the child's birthday.
  • CDA co-matching caps for children born between 1 Jan 2015 and 31 Mar 2027 are kept until 30 Sep 2027, then move to the uniform $5,000 cap.
  • The PSEA top-up of $10,000 goes to children turning 17 from 2026 onwards; those who turned 17 in 2026 receive it in June 2027.
  • Large Family LifeSG Credits ($1,000 a year for a third or later child aged 1 to 6) end after the 2026 tranche, as does the extra $5,000 CDA First Step Grant for large families. The $5,000 Large Family MediSave Grant stays.

Where to find the real numbers

CDA — your bank's CDA statement, or the Baby Bonus Online portal. Log each deposit you make: the Government match is worked out from them.

LifeSG credits — "My Benefits" in the LifeSG app, which shows each tranche's exact expiry date.

Edusave and PSEA — the MOE Edusave and PSEA statements. Unused Edusave moves to the PSEA in the year your child turns 17.

MediSave — your child's CPF statement. MediShield Life premiums are deducted automatically each year.

Use Other credit or correction to bring a balance in line with a statement.
