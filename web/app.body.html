Only available within 12 months of a child's birth — maternity leave can start up to 4 weeks before. Once that window closes, the leave no longer applies and the child drops off this list. Counted in working days; 5 days make a week.

Where to find the real numbers

CDA — your bank's CDA statement, or the Baby Bonus Online portal. Log each deposit you make: the Government match is worked out from them.

LifeSG credits — "My Benefits" in the LifeSG app, which shows each tranche's exact expiry date.

Edusave and PSEA — the MOE statements. Unused Edusave moves to the PSEA in the year your child turns 17.

MediSave — your child's CPF statement. MediShield Life premiums are deducted automatically each year.

Use Other credit or correction to bring a balance in line with a statement.
