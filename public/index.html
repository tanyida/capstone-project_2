What changed

Transition rules

For children born before 1 April 2027, who are not starting from scratch.

  • Children still receiving Baby Bonus Cash Gift payouts keep them until 31 Mar 2027, then get a top-up by 30 Apr 2027 bringing the total to $10,000.
  • Child Credits are backdated. Children who turn 1 to 16 in 2026 receive their 2026 tranche of $2,000 by 30 Apr 2027; children with January to April birthdays also get their 2027 tranche by then. From 2028 the credits arrive on the child's birthday.
  • CDA co-matching caps for children born between 1 Jan 2015 and 31 Mar 2027 are kept until 30 Sep 2027, then move to the uniform $5,000 cap.
  • The PSEA top-up of $10,000 goes to children turning 17 from 2026 onwards; those who turned 17 in 2026 receive it in June 2027.
  • Discontinued for large families: the extra $5,000 CDA First Step Grant and the $6,000 Large Family LifeSG Credits for the third and later child. The $5,000 Large Family MediSave Grant stays.
